Straight answers

Questions people actually ask

If the answer you need isn't here, call us at (843) 655-4121 and ask.

What is OnGuard, exactly?

A membership. You pay a flat fee, we come to your property two or four times a year and perform the same eleven maintenance items every visit, then send you a photo report of what we found. It's the routine upkeep most people mean to do and don't.

Is this a home inspection or a home warranty?

Neither. It's routine preventative maintenance plus a visual condition report. It is not a home inspection under South Carolina law and isn't performed by a licensed home inspector, and it doesn't pay to replace things after they break the way a warranty does — we're trying to keep them from breaking.

My house doesn't have everything on the list.

Then we skip those items. No garbage disposal, a tankless water heater, no dryer hookup — you're buying the visit and the report rather than a count of tasks.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most members aren't. We need safe access — a code, a lockbox, or a key on file — and somewhere to put the pets. You'll know the month up front and the exact day 48 hours ahead.

What does it cost?

Two visits a year is $30 a month, or $329 paid annually. Four visits a year is $52 a month, or $569 annually, plus an adjustment for extra HVAC systems. Larger properties are quoted after the enrollment visit. Filter and bulb labor is included; materials are billed at our cost, and detector batteries are on us.

What's the enrollment visit?

A one-time $99 walk-through where we record every filter size, find your shutoffs and panel, and photograph the baseline. Waived if we've done work for you in the last two years.

Is there a contract?

Memberships run for a twelve-month term and renew automatically unless you tell us otherwise. The full terms are in the membership agreement you'll sign at enrollment.

What happens if you find a problem?

It goes in the report under fix now, this year, or watch. If it needs a quote, we send one, and we don't do work you haven't approved. The report is yours — you're not obliged to use us for the repair, though members get 10% off our labor and no service-call fee when they do.

Do you cover condos and second homes?

Yes, and second homes are where this earns its keep. We check the place while you're away and send photos, so you're not finding out in June what happened in February.

How long does a visit take?

About 90 minutes for a typical home. Bigger houses and multiple HVAC systems take longer.

What if I need to reschedule?

Tell us before the day and we'll move you to the next date that zone runs. If we show up and can't get in, we'll reschedule once at no charge — a second missed visit counts as used.

Can I call you between visits?

That's part of it. Members get worked in ahead of the general schedule, and we waive the service-call fee. You're not waiting behind everyone who called that morning.

Why would I pay monthly if you only come twice a year?

The monthly amount is the yearly price split into payments, the same as an insurance premium or an HVAC agreement. Pay for the year up front instead and you save about a month.

When am I charged?

Annual members are charged at signing and on each anniversary. Monthly members are charged the same day each month, by card or ACH, with a receipt emailed each time.

Does it transfer if I sell the house?

Not automatically — it covers one property and one owner. Tell us you're selling and we'll talk about handing it to the buyer, which makes a good closing gift.

What don't you do?

Garage doors, anything on the roof, crawlspace entry, appliance repair, pest control, and any licensed trade we don't hold — HVAC refrigerant work, electrical past bulbs, gas. Gutters and pressure washing are available as add-ons.
